## Learnability Section — ROAS, ACOS, TACOS

### ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
ROAS measures how much revenue a brand earns for every rupee spent on advertising.

**Formula:** ROAS = Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend

**Example:** GlowNest spends ₹3,00,000 on ads and earns ₹12,00,000 in ad sales → ROAS = 4

A ROAS of 4 means every ₹1 spent on ads returns ₹4 in revenue. For premium skincare, a ROAS above 3 is generally healthy. It varies by category because low-margin products need higher ROAS to stay profitable, while high-margin products can survive lower ROAS.

---

### ACOS (Advertising Cost of Sale)
ACOS is the exact inverse of ROAS. It tells what percentage of ad revenue was spent on advertising.

**Formula:** ACOS = Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue × 100

**Example:** ₹3,00,000 spend ÷ ₹12,00,000 revenue × 100 = 25% ACOS

ROAS 4 = ACOS 25% — they are mirror images of each other.

A high ACOS is acceptable when launching a new product (buying visibility) but becomes a problem when the brand is mature and needs to be profitable. For GlowNest at 25% ACOS, this is healthy because their gross margin is 65%.

---

### TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sale)
TACOS is the real health metric because it measures ad spend against TOTAL revenue — both paid and organic sales combined.

**Formula:** TACOS = Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue (organic + paid) × 100

**Example:** ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹28,00,000 × 100 = 10.7%

As a brand grows organically, TACOS drops even if ad spend stays the same. This means the brand is building real equity on the platform — customers find it without needing an ad push. TACOS below 15% is considered healthy. GlowNest at 10.7% is in a strong position.

### Q1 — Unit Economics

### Q2 — Monthly Profitability

| Source | Calculation | Amount |
|--------|------------|--------|
| Profit from Ad Units | ₹224 × 1,500 | ₹3,36,000 |
| Profit from Organic Units | ₹424 × 2,000 | ₹8,48,000 |
| Less Fixed Costs | | -₹1,50,000 |
| **Total Monthly Profit** | | **₹10,34,000** |

Organic Net Margin = ₹520 - ₹96 = ₹424 (no advertising cost on organic sales)

---

### Q3 — ACOS and TACOS

- **ACOS** = ₹3,00,000 ÷ (₹800 × 1,500) × 100 = **25%** ✅ Healthy
- **TACOS** = ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹28,00,000 × 100 = **10.7%** ✅ Very healthy

**Assessment:** Both metrics are strong. At 25% ACOS with a 65% gross margin, GlowNest is comfortably profitable on every ad-driven sale. TACOS at 10.7% shows that organic sales are doing significant work — 57% of revenue requires zero ad spend. This brand is in a healthy growth phase, not dependent on ads to survive.

---

### Q4 — Break-Even Ad Spend

At break-even, Total Monthly Profit = 0

Organic profit = ₹424 × 2,000 = ₹8,48,000
Fixed costs = ₹1,50,000
Organic profit after fixed costs = ₹6,98,000

This means ad-attributed sales can produce a loss of up to ₹6,98,000 before the business goes negative.

Ad units contribute ₹320 gross (₹520 - ₹96) per unit before ad cost.
Break-even ad spend = ₹320 × 1,500 + ₹6,98,000 = **₹13,78,000/month**

**Corresponding ACOS** = ₹13,78,000 ÷ ₹12,00,000 × 100 = **114.8%**

This shows how strong the organic base is — it subsidises a very large ad spend before the business loses money.

### Q5 — ACOS Worsens to 40%, Units Drop to 1,200

- New ad spend = 40% × (₹800 × 1,200) = **₹3,84,000**
- New ad cost per unit = ₹3,84,000 ÷ 1,200 = ₹320
- New net margin per ad unit = ₹520 - ₹96 - ₹320 = **₹104**
- New monthly profit = (₹104 × 1,200) + (₹424 × 2,000) - ₹1,50,000 = **₹8,22,800**

Profit dropped by ₹2,11,200 vs current.

**Recommendation:** Pause the lowest performing keywords immediately. Run a negative keyword audit to stop ad spend leaking on irrelevant searches. Concentrate budget on the top 10 converting keywords only. Do not increase overall spend until ACOS recovers below 30%.
